R-physics Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026.

This policy describes how R-physics for Android (package com.sewqert.rphysics) handles data. The developer listed on Google Play is ВАЛЕНТИНА ЄВГЕНІВНА КУЛИНИЧ. You can play without creating an account. The app does not contain ads or use in-app analytics.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your settings, game progress, nickname, and the last room address you entered so they are available the next time you play. When you import a map or vehicle through Android’s file picker, the app reads the file you select and stores a copy for use in the game. Screenshots you take in the game are saved on your device.

If the app crashes, it may save a technical log on your device with details about the error and recent game activity. The app does not automatically send this log to the developer. Android and Google Play may handle diagnostics separately according to your device settings and Google’s policies.

Local multiplayer

When you host or join a room, devices exchange data directly over the network. The app uses network addresses to connect devices and sends nicknames, room details, game state, and chat messages to support room discovery and multiplayer. Players looking for rooms on the same network may see the room name and the host’s nickname. Room participants may receive copies of the selected map and the custom vehicle files used in the room. Received files may remain in players’ local caches.

Rooms and chat run without a separate server operated by the developer. The app does not encrypt local multiplayer traffic.

Online map catalog and external links

When you open the map catalog or download a map from it, the app makes an HTTPS request to GitHub. GitHub receives your IP address and technical request information needed to serve the file. If you open a link to Telegram or Discord, that service handles your data under its own policies.

Retention and deletion

Settings, progress, imported files, cached files, and logs stored by the app stay on your device until the app or you remove them. You can clear R-physics data in Android settings or uninstall the app. Original files, screenshots in a shared folder, and copies that other players have already received are stored separately.

If you email the developer, your email address, message, and any attachments you send are used to respond and investigate the issue. This information is kept for as long as needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask the developer to delete your correspondence using the address below.

Contact

For questions about privacy or to request deletion of your data, email flopsikgames@gmail.com. If the way the app handles data changes, the developer will update this page and the effective date.
